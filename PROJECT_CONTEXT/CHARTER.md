# Project charter

## Identity

- **Working title:** Overclock Simulator
- **Platform:** Roblox
- **Audience:** Casual Roblox players, especially mobile players.
- **Experience:** A cozy, low-graphics desk-lab simulator where a player grows one CPU over time.

## Purpose

Give players the satisfying feeling of owning a small machine that keeps improving:

> Build a CPU -> let it compute -> collect Compute -> upgrade it -> return to see progress.

The game must be understandable in under 30 seconds and provide the first meaningful upgrade within five minutes.

## Design principles

- Simple before deep.
- Visible cause and effect.
- Short, relaxing sessions and rewarding returns.
- Low visual complexity; readable UI, color, light, and small effects matter more than high-detail graphics.
- Cooperative social comparison, not hostile competition.
- Server-authoritative economy and progression.
- Honest verification over optimistic claims.

## Non-negotiables

- No pay-to-win mechanics.
- All functional CPU progression is earnable without payment.
- Gamepasses may provide convenience or cosmetics only.
- No required purchases, paid random rewards, exclusive maximum stats, or premium-only resources.
- Do not add complexity merely to imitate other simulator games.

## Explicitly out of scope for v1

- Pets
- Trading
- Rebirths
- Combat
- Large open world
- Random paid progression
- Heavy graphics
- Complex crafting systems

# Social systems

**Decision:** Players may visit and compare CPU desks; visitors cannot alter another player's machine or resources.

**Proposal:** A global rotating component shop and future Power Cycle announcements provide shared discoveries without hostile competition.

**Inference:** Cooperative alerts and safe comparison can create conversation around a simple loop.

# Simulator design research

**Inference:** A clear repeatable loop, visible progress, and a reason to return can make a small simulator feel rewarding without high visual complexity.

**Decision:** Overclock Simulator uses offline Compute accumulation with a capacity limit, rotating component discovery, safe desk comparison, lightweight visual feedback, and sustainable update scope.

**Decision:** Functional progression remains free. Optional purchases may provide cosmetics or convenience only.
